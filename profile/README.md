# TheHSI
**TheHSI is a Team of mildly good programmers trying to make a new standard in technology**

## What do we do?
We are an open-source software development organization dedicated to building free, accessible, and innovative technology for everyone.

We develop software, tools, infrastructure, and services that aim to solve real problems while remaining open, transparent, and accessible. Our projects are built around the belief that useful technology should not be locked behind unnecessary barriers.

Our mission is to empower developers and communities by sharing knowledge, building useful tools, and creating opportunities for collaboration. We believe that by working openly and together, we can help create a better, more independent digital world.

Whether we are building a small utility, a large platform, or experimenting with new ideas, we focus on learning, improving, and making technology that people can actually use.
